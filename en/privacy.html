Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026 (revised for Google sign-in)

HARU TRIP (the "Site") complies with Korea's Personal Information Protection Act and other applicable laws and treats your personal information with care. This policy explains what information the Site collects, why, and how it is managed. If this English version differs from the Korean version, the Korean version prevails.

1. Information we collect and why

Anyone can read travel information and reviews without signing in. Liking spots, posting reviews and recommending places require signing in with a Google account. The Site never receives or stores your password.

Reviews and recommendations are visible to all visitors along with the nickname you enter — nothing else from your account is shown. Please do not include your real name, contact details or other personal information in your posts.

2. Retention

Sign-in information, reviews, recommendations and likes are kept until you request account deletion or the Site shuts down, and are then destroyed without delay. Deleting your account also deletes the reviews, recommendations and likes made with it. Anonymous reviews and likes left before sign-in was introduced are kept for the same period.

5. Your rights

When signed in, you can delete your own reviews and recommendations directly on the Site. To view, correct or delete your personal information, or to delete your account, email us from the Google account you signed in with and we will handle it without delay after verification. For anonymous reviews posted before sign-in was introduced, tell us the location (city and spot), nickname and date posted.

You can also remove the Site's access in your Google Account third-party connections.
