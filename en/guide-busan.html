🚃 Course A: Blueline Ocean & Haeundae Romance Course

⏱️ About 5–6 hours📍 About 6.8 km

Emerald seas and the Sky Capsule along the old coastal railway

Suggested route: Haeundae Blueline Park & Sky Capsule → Cheongsapo Daritdol Skywalk & Coast → Haeundae Beach & Dongbaekseom Island (Nurimaru)

A romantic capsule gliding between sky and sea, and the charm of Cheongsapo

1. Haeundae Blueline Park & Sky Capsule

해운대 블루라인파크 & 스카이캡슐 · Coastal tourist train / Unique activity

Haeundae Blueline Park is an eco-friendly tourist railway running 4.8 km from Mipo in Haeundae through Dalmaji Tunnel, Cheongsapo, the Daritdol Skywalk and Gudeokpo to Songjeong. The 4-seat "Sky Capsule," which rolls slowly at 5 km/h along a rail 7–10 m above the ground, offers a private view of the East Sea right below your feet and is hugely popular with travelers. Pair it with the stylish cafés around Mipo Station.

Suggested time
about 1.5–2 hours
Address
13 Dalmaji-gil 62beon-gil, Haeundae-gu, Busan (Mipo Station)
Travel tip
Ride the Sky Capsule from Mipo toward Cheongsapo (west to east) to use the seaward rail for wider ocean views. Book online in advance.

2. Cheongsapo Daritdol Skywalk & Coast

청사포 다릿돌전망대 & 해안가 · Sea skywalk / Fishing village

The Cheongsapo Daritdol Skywalk sits along the old Mipo railway between Cheongsapo and Songjeong. "Daritdol" refers to the reefs that line up like stepping stones from the Cheongsapo shore out to sea. Walking on the half-moon glass floor at the end feels like stepping out over the water. Afterwards, relax over Cheongsapo's famous charcoal-grilled clams or at an ocean-view café.

Suggested time
about 40 minutes–1 hour
Address
San 3-9, Jung-dong, Haeundae-gu, Busan
Travel tip
Entry may be restricted on very windy days, so check the weather. Entry is free with shoe covers, and the sunset view is spectacular.

3. Haeundae Beach & Dongbaekseom Island (Nurimaru)

해운대 해수욕장 & 동백섬 누리마루 · Famous beach / Coastal walk

With 1.5 km of fine sand and clear water, Haeundae Beach is truly Korea's top beach. Dongbaekseom at its western end was once an island but is now joined to the mainland by sand deposits, with a well-built coastal deck road running through camellia and pine trees. The viewpoints around Nurimaru APEC House, where world leaders once met, overlook Gwangan Bridge and the Oryukdo islets.

Suggested time
about 1.5 hours
Address
116 Dongbaek-ro, U-dong, Haeundae-gu, Busan
Travel tip
Follow the Dongbaekseom deck trail past the mermaid statue and the lighthouse viewpoint to Nurimaru. Don't miss grilled hagfish and tteokbokki at Haeundae Market.

🎨 Course B: Old Downtown Retro & Yeongdo Huinnyeoul Course

⏱️ About 6–7 hours📍 About 7.5 km

Busan's living history, a cliffside "Santorini" village and lively Jagalchi Market

Suggested route: Yeongdo Huinnyeoul Culture Village & Jeoryeong Coastal Tunnel → Jagalchi Fish Market & BIFF Square Food Alley → Gamcheon Culture Village & Little Prince Photo Spot

Seaside cliff alleys of Yeongdo and the warm hearts of Busan locals

1. Yeongdo Huinnyeoul Culture Village & Jeoryeong Coastal Tunnel

영도 흰여울문화마을 & 절영해안터널 · Seaside cliff village / Film location

Huinnyeoul Culture Village began as a cliff-edge neighborhood of Korean War refugees and is now one of Busan's most charming spots, nurtured by artists and residents. White walls and pastel houses blend with the blue sea of Namhang, and the lanes are full of small gift shops and ocean-view terrace cafés. Head down to the Jeoryeong Coastal Walk below the village to find the Huinnyeoul Coastal Tunnel, a favorite photo spot framing the sea.

Suggested time
about 2 hours
Address
194 Jeoryeong-ro, Yeongdo-gu, Busan
Travel tip
The most famous shot at the coastal tunnel is a backlit silhouette looking out to the sea from inside. There are many stairs, so wear comfortable sneakers.

2. Jagalchi Fish Market & BIFF Square Food Alley

자갈치시장 & BIFF 광장 미식골목 · Traditional fish market / Street food

Jagalchi is Korea's largest fish market and a symbol of Busan, full of fresh seafood and the friendly dialect of the "Jagalchi ajimae" fishwives. Pick live fish on the first floor of the seafood center and have it prepared upstairs, or enjoy grilled mackerel and hagfish in the market alleys. Cross the underpass to BIFF (Busan International Film Festival) Square, a food paradise with handprints of film stars, the original nut-filled seed hotteok, spicy glass noodles and stuffed tofu-pouch hot pot.

Suggested time
about 1.5–2 hours
Address
52 Jagalchihaean-ro, Jung-gu, Busan
Travel tip
Go up to the "Jagalchi Sky Park" observatory on the market building's roof for a free 360-degree view of Namhang, Yeongdo Bridge and Busan Tower.

3. Gamcheon Culture Village & Little Prince Photo Spot

감천문화마을 & 어린왕자 포토존 · Terraced art village / Photo spot

Gamcheon Culture Village is a terraced village built on a steep hillside by refugees in the 1950s. Its houses were built in orderly tiers so none blocks the view of the one behind. Artists and residents later filled the lanes with colorful murals and sculptures, turning it into a world-famous attraction. The statue of the Little Prince and the Desert Fox sitting on a railing overlooking the village always has a line of travelers waiting for a photo.

Suggested time
about 1.5 hours
Address
203 Gamnae 2-ro, Saha-gu, Busan
Travel tip
Buy a stamp-tour map at the village information center to efficiently find the hidden galleries and rooftop viewpoints.

🌉 Course C: Trendy Ocean Life & Gwangan Bridge "Diamond" Night Course

⏱️ About 5–6 hours📍 About 5.2 km

Youthful Gwangalli Beach, the trendy Millac the Market and a stunning drone show

Suggested route: Gwangalli Beach & Terrace Café Street → Millac the Market → Gwangan Bridge "Diamond" Night View & M Drone Light Show

Hot spots and the buzzing night sea against the Diamond Bridge

1. Gwangalli Beach & Terrace Café Street

광안리 해수욕장 & 테라스 카페거리 · Youthful beach / Ocean-view cafés

Gwangalli is one of Busan's signature beaches where city and sea meet most harmoniously. It offers the best head-on view of Gwangan Bridge crossing the sea, and with clean water and a wide beach it's lively all year round. Cafés with floor-to-ceiling windows, brunch spots and craft beer pubs line the shore, making it perfect for a relaxed ocean lifestyle.

Suggested time
about 1.5 hours
Address
219 Gwanganhaebyeon-ro, Suyeong-gu, Busan
Travel tip
Try a forced-perspective photo that makes Gwangan Bridge sit on your palm, by the beach sculptures or on the sand.

2. Millac the Market

밀락더마켓 (Millac the Market) · Trendy cultural complex / Ocean-view stand

Millac the Market is a red-brick, warehouse-style cultural complex near the former Millak Waterfront Park. Spanning about 7,700 m² from the basement to the second floor, its centerpiece is a giant "ocean-view stand" of stepped seating facing huge windows with views of Gwangan Bridge and Millak Port. Craft beer, doughnuts, lobster sandwiches and more are on offer, along with select shops, regular busking and art exhibitions.

Suggested time
about 1–1.5 hours
Address
56 Millaksubyeon-ro 17beon-gil, Suyeong-gu, Busan
Travel tip
Grab takeout from the food stalls and settle into the ocean-view stand for an indoor seaside picnic.

3. Gwangan Bridge "Diamond" Night View & M Drone Light Show

광안대교 다이아몬드 야경 & M 드론라이트쇼 · Landmark night view / Drone light show

Gwangan Bridge is a 7,420 m double-deck bridge over the sea. At night its LED lighting system displays over 100,000 colors, earning it the nickname "Diamond Bridge." The head-on view from Gwangalli Beach is considered the highlight of any Busan trip. Every Saturday evening, hundreds of drones draw 3D images and messages in the night sky to music in the "Gwangalli M Drone Light Show," amazing visitors from around the world.

Suggested time
about 1.5–2 hours
Address
219 Gwanganhaebyeon-ro, Suyeong-gu, Busan (Gwangalli Beach area)
Travel tip
The drone show runs every Saturday evening (winter 7 pm/9 pm, summer 8 pm/10 pm). Lay out a mat in the middle of the beach 30 minutes early for the best view.
Opening hours, fees and access restrictions can change with the season and local conditions. Please check each place's official information before you visit.
